FAQ

Can we set our own retail prices?
Yes. Our retail prices are recommendations only. Every shop and web shop sets its own price.
Where do the keys work?
Croatian keys activate in all EU/EEA countries. Non-EU keys activate only in Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro and North Macedonia, which protects your sales from cheap grey-market keys.
Will the price change after release?
Yes, on a fixed schedule: launch prices from March to May 2027, lower prices from June 2027 and from March 2028, and short sales from September 2028. Each sale price is a real reduction from the price of the previous 30 days.
Do you offer volume discounts?
No. Every shop pays the same price per key. Sale prices apply only to keys ordered during a sale, up to the shop's average monthly orders.
